# Research sources

## Status of the seed content

The 16 creatures, 18 legends, 10 published themes and 4 lessons in the seed were drafted from standard, well-known sources and scholarship, **without access to the texts during drafting** (the build environment could reach package registries only). Everything is therefore `provisional`. Specifically:

- **Locators** are book-level (e.g. Pliny *NH* Book 8, Isidore *Etym.* XII.2) except where a precise reference is very well established (Pliny 8.1 for the elephant; Caesar *BG* 6.27; biblical verses). Chapter-level locators still need to be added from editions.
- **Physiologus chapters** are cited by name ("Lion chapter"), because chapter numbering differs between versions and translations.
- **No folio numbers** are recorded for any manuscript witness. Each witness says "folio to be confirmed".
- **Manuscript families**: Aberdeen, Ashmole 1511, Bodley 764 and Morgan M.81 are recorded as Second family with evidence "supported"; Cambridge Ii.4.26, Harley 4751, Royal 12 C XIX and the Northumberland Bestiary are "to be confirmed".
- **Dates** follow the conventional ranges in the scholarship and should be checked against current catalogue records.
- **Location coordinates** are approximate (city level) and marked unverified.
- **Claims with no citation** (mostly modern zoology statements) display "Citation needed". Each needs a zoological reference.
